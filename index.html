About Me

I am a student of Environmental Science and Disaster Management at Daffodil International University, with a strong foundation in remote sensing, GIS and environmental impact assessment. I use scientific research and data analysis to work on climate resilience, water and health, and sustainable development, with a focus on communities in coastal Bangladesh.
