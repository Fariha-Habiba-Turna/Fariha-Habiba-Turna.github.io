About Me

Hello! I'm Fariha Habiba Turna, a student of Environmental Science and Disaster Management, with a keen interest in understanding and addressing the environmental challenges of our time.

Currently studying at Daffodil International University, I am passionate about applying my knowledge to promote sustainable practices and enhance resilience against environmental risks. My academic focus includes climate change, sustainable resource management, disaster risk reduction, and remote sensing and GIS. I use scientific research and data analysis to contribute to solutions for environmental resilience and sustainable development.

This site is a platform to share my research, projects and insights. I look forward to engaging with fellow students, researchers and professionals dedicated to a more sustainable future.
